ABOUT SHIPRATE

Shipping clarity,
before checkout.

ShipRate checks courier availability and estimated charges using the Shiprocket serviceability API. No shipment is booked and no order is created.

How estimates work

Enter pickup and delivery pincodes, actual parcel weight, payment mode, and order value. The app sends the supported route, weight, and COD parameters to Shiprocket and displays the returned courier options.

Estimates depend on your Shiprocket account and may change with shipment attributes, surcharges, and final booking details.
